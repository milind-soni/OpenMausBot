Ask a bot to change how the app looks, for example "make my app look like a 90s terminal" or "give the chat a slow moving starry background". The bot makes a **theme** and your open windows switch to it at once, on the desktop app and the web app. Phones keep their own look.

A theme only changes the look for you. On a team, your teammates keep theirs.

## What a theme can change

- **Colours.** A background and an accent colour. The app works out the rest: surfaces, lines and text that stay readable. Single colours can be set too, separately for light and dark.
- **Fonts.** The fonts that come with the app, fonts installed on your computer, or a font file the theme carries.
- **Anything else, with CSS.** Borders, roundness, spacing, message bubble shapes, the sidebar, the message box, the chat header, animated backgrounds, hover effects.
- **Pictures.** The bot can add images from its own folder or from files you attach to the chat, including animated GIF and WebP, for backgrounds and textures.

The bot cannot see the result. It makes small changes and asks you how it looks. Every change has **Undo** on the card in the chat.

## Nothing loads from the internet

A theme's CSS could otherwise send what is on your screen to another site. So a theme loads only its own pictures and fonts, kept with your workspace, and pictures written into the CSS itself. Anything else is removed before the CSS is used: links to other sites, `@import`, `image-set()` and escaped characters that hide them.

## Settings → Appearance

Under **Your themes** you can use, edit, rename, duplicate and delete themes, and change a theme's colours and CSS. **Export** saves a theme with its pictures as one file to share; **Import** adds one. **Reset look** goes back to the default look.

## Getting back

If a theme hides something you need:

- Press **Cmd+Option+Shift+R** on a Mac or **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R** on Windows and Linux. It works anywhere in the app and goes back to the default look.
- On the web app, open the address with `?safe-look` at the end, such as `https://you.mausbot.cloud/?safe-look`. That window starts without your theme.
- Ask the bot to switch back to a built-in look.
