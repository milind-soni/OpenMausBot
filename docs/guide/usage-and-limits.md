# Usage left

The small rings at the top of the window show how much usage is left on the AI apps your bots use. A fuller ring means more usage is left. Click a ring for details, or move over it with your mouse. You can also reach each ring with Tab and open it with Enter or Space.

- Green means more than 40% is left.
- Amber means 15–40% is left. A small dot also marks low usage.
- Red means less than 15% is left. An exclamation mark draws attention to it.
- “Out of usage” means the AI app reported that work cannot continue right now.
- A dashed ring with a question mark means the amount left is not reported or the reading is too old. It does not mean the account is empty.

Some AI apps have more than one allowance, such as a few hours and a week. The ring shows the lowest amount left. Open it to see every reported allowance. “Refreshes at” uses your computer’s local clock. A refresh time is an estimate from the AI app, not a promise that work will restart then.

Codex can report usage left without asking an AI model to do work. While the window is open and Codex is idle, the app checks at most once every five minutes. It also receives readings during conversations. Other AI apps may report only that usage has run out. Claude’s amount left is currently shown as unknown because its numbers have not been verified. Grok and Antigravity also remain unknown. Gerer Build Studio never sends a message to an AI model just to check usage.

Readings older than 15 minutes stop showing a percentage. An allowance whose refresh time has passed also stops showing its old percentage until a fresh reading arrives. “Updated” tells you when information was last received.

In a narrow window, the rings become one summary. It shows the AI app that needs attention first. Open it to see the others.

“Set a backup model…” currently opens the bot’s settings. Automatic switching and waiting to continue are being added separately; this display does not enable them. You can choose another AI model in bot settings if you want to continue now.

To hide the rings, open Settings → Models & Providers and turn off “Show usage in the top bar.” You can also hide individual AI apps there. These settings only change the display.

Some AI companies call these allowances "rate limits" or "usage quotas".
